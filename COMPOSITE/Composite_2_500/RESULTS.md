# Composite_2_500 · Composite plate, m = 2, n = 500

m = 2 ply angles, n = 500 training points, 19500 test points, Gaussian kernel, constant trend. Harmonized specification (see [`_scripts/README.md`](../../_scripts/README.md)).

Libraries (10): `DACE`, `egobox-gp`, `GPflow`, `GPML`, `GPstuff`, `GPyTorch`, `OpenTURNS`, `SMT`, `tinygp`, `UQLab`.
